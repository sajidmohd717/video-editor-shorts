# Shot list — lf-001

Paired with `script.md`, which now contains **spoken narration only**.

**Why they're separate:** an earlier version mixed editing notes into the script.
The TTS reader can't reliably strip multi-line bracketed notes, so notes would
have been read aloud — and on a paid per-character API you find that out after
paying for it. Narration file is spoken text and nothing else. Always.

## Ch1 — The question

- **Cold open:** news montage, **source audio**, 3–4 clips, ~8s total. Anchors
  saying the numbers aloud, chyrons visible. Per L5 this is the one place source
  audio leads — it establishes the story as externally reported before we claim
  anything.
- Numbers appear as spoken: "$100B", "$300B", "$638B" — serif on black (L7 card
  style).
- Hold on black at "they start to lead to each other." Let it sit.

## Ch2 — One deal

- **OpenAI's announcement page**, 22 Sept 2025. Highlight-sweep each qualifier as
  it's spoken: *letter of intent* → *intends to* → *up to* → *progressively as
  deployed*. Four separate sweeps, one per phrase.
- Cut to NVIDIA's newsroom page showing the same wording.
- **No cutting** through "the number that travelled was a hundred billion
  dollars." Thesis landing.

## Ch3 — The same shape

- **Anthropic's announcement**, highlighted on "up to ten billion" and "up to
  five billion".
- Same page, highlight "$30 billion of Azure compute".
- **Simple diagram:** two boxes, arrows both ways. Investment out, compute
  purchase back. Build it as the line is spoken.
- Keynote/interview footage under the "not fraud" paragraph — being fair needs a
  human face, not a document.

## Ch4 — The filings *(strongest chapter — slow down)*

- **Oracle Q4 FY2026 release**, RPO line highlighted.
- **Chart:** RPO over time, vertical. $553B → $638B.
- **Full-screen quote card**, white serif on black, held through the whole line:
  the prepaid and customer-supplied hardware sentence. Attribute on screen to
  "Oracle, Q4 FY2026 release" — the release names no executive, so no face and no
  name (verified).
- Return to the document for "sitting in a public document almost nobody read."

## Ch5 — The other side

- Executive interview/keynote footage making the capital-intensive argument.
  **One short source-audio clip** here — hearing them say it is the point.
- Archival: telecoms build-out, late 1990s. Brief, 2–3s.
- **Three-item card**, items appearing as spoken.
- Final card held to the end. **No end card, no subscribe prompt** (F17).
- **No source audio anywhere in this chapter after the one clip** — L5: the final
  third carries the argument alone.
